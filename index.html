🛡️ Anti-Counterfeit & Tactile Verification Guide

The RBI MANI app only checks visual patterns and cannot identify fake notes. Sahaaya AI provides verified tactile marks and security checks for visually impaired users:

  • ✅ Tactile Bleed Lines: 5 angular bleed lines on left and right for ₹500 (circle shape for ₹500, triangle for ₹100).
  • ✅ Security Thread: Color shifts from green to blue when tilted.
  • ✅ Watermark: Electrotype Mahatma Gandhi portrait visible against light.
  • ✅ Intaglio Print: Raised printing of Ashoka Pillar emblem and RBI seal.

🏛️ Major Government Aid Schemes & Application Portals

1. ADIP Scheme (Assistance to Disabled Persons)

Provides 100% free or subsidized smart canes, braille refreshers, hearing aids, wheelchairs, and pediatric cochlear implants for families with monthly income under ₹15,000.

Visit ADIP Portal ↗
2. UDID (Unique Disability ID) Card Portal

Universal single identity card for persons with disabilities, enabling travel concessions, scholarships, and healthcare benefits across all Indian states.

Apply on Swavlamban Portal ↗
3. Sugamya Bharat Abhiyan (Accessible India Campaign)

Drives accessibility retrofitting across Indian railway stations, public buildings, and central government digital portals under the RPwD Act 2016.

4. ALIMCO (Artificial Limbs Manufacturing Corporation of India)

Organizes nationwide distribution camps for assistive devices. National Toll-Free Helpline: 1800-180-5129.
